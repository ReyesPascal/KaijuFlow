# Remaining live-service validation gaps

Status: **unknown outside isolated fixtures**, 2026-11-07. A4 is a closed bounded synthetic milestone. The separate `INT-DISCOVERY-STREAM-001` passed [CI run 42373119985](https://github.com/ReyesPascal/KaijuFlow/actions/runs/42373119985), job `128405137873`, and delivered 64 exact synthetic bytes; it did not play a video. Evidence: `EV-KF-INT-001`, [permanent result](../../../evidence/results/INT-DISCOVERY-STREAM-001.json), [integration boundaries](INTEGRATION-DISCOVERY-STREAM.md). WebDAV listing size was fabricated as 128 MiB to exercise original file-selection rules. These results cannot establish real-service performance.

All future validation below is **proposed**, requires separately authorized non-production infrastructure, and is not authorized for execution by this report. Do not use Brain to close these gaps. Do not add another synthetic campaign to the completed assignment.

| Gap | Missing evidence | Why synthetic fixtures are insufficient | Minimum future validation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Real NNTP provider behavior and throughput | Sustained decoded bytes/s; real authentication/TLS, quotas, retention, latency, incomplete articles and connection limits | Local fake sockets return predetermined responses without provider scheduling, bandwidth, storage or policy constraints; STAT samples do not prove complete retrieval | On separately authorized staging, retrieve a legal controlled corpus through approved providers; capture per-provider connections, article errors, decode throughput and latency, keeping credentials outside reports |
| Real NzbDAV job processing | Actual queue progression, retrieval, repair/unpack, completed-file availability and job failure handling | Integration fake immediately reports completion and serves prearranged bytes; no article retrieval or archive extraction occurs | Pin NzbDAV build/settings on staging; submit a small legal complete NZB and a controlled incomplete source; correlate queue/history transitions, resulting object hash and WebDAV availability |
| Original-quality 4K playback | Byte/codec/container identity and sustained actual player decoding on the target device | A title containing 2160p and a successful HTTP response do not demonstrate a decodable video or its original bitrate | Play legally controlled original 4K material with known hash, codecs, duration and bitrate trace on the intended player; verify passthrough identity and record a sustained playback trace |
| Time to first frame | Player-observed first decoded/presented frame relative to user play action | HTTP first byte, preparation completion and fabricated metadata do not include player buffering, demux, decoder initialization or display | Instrument a fixed player/device with play-action and first-frame timestamps; correlate request, submission, preparation and first-byte milestones for cold and warm starts |
| Seeking and buffering during sustained playback | Seek-to-frame latency, offset correctness, discontinuities and rebuffer events over time | Bounded 200/206/416 fixtures and a 64-byte response do not exercise sparse range patterns, long buffers, player read-ahead or midstream source stalls | At least 30 minutes per defined playback trial with scripted forward/backward seeks, including near-end offsets; record resumed frame timestamp, Range/body identity, buffering duration and upstream reconnects |
| Concurrent playback and resource use | Scaling curves, peak NNTP connections, CPU, RSS, disk read/write/latency and network utilization per active stream | Tiny isolated requests do not establish real buffer occupancy, disk contention, provider quotas or long-lived socket demand | Repeat the same defined source workload at 1, 2 and 4 simultaneous staging players; collect synchronized per-process/host/resource traces and report capacity failures rather than changing production |
| Provider recovery under real network conditions | Detection time, retry/backoff behavior, fallback success and stream continuity during loss, throttling and recovery | Deterministic loopback failures omit WAN jitter, half-open TLS sockets, provider rate limits and correlated source failures | On staging only, repeat controlled outage/throttle/restore trials for an approved provider/source; measure recovery deadlines, fallback choice, missing/duplicated bytes and player rebuffering |
| Full archive compatibility | End-to-end supported-format coverage across multipart, encrypted, solid, compressed, obfuscated, corrupt and unsupported archives | Signature detection, grouping and small header/yEnc tests do not perform exhaustive extraction, repair, password handling or seek mapping | Use a legal generated/labeled archive corpus with expected entries/hashes; record format/method/version/password boundaries, full processing outcomes and explicit unsupported cases; never infer universal compatibility |
