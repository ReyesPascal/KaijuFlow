Worth knowing

  • altHUB was out for everyone. Its daily limit was reached before the evening run (HTTP 429 on every search), so all three sides searched NinjaCentral and DrunkenSlug only. That is why the Source counts are lower than in the afternoon. The comparison between sides is still fair.
  • The Unscramble second. It is the one place where KaijuFlow with AltMount is slower than today's setup, and only on the very first play of a file. If that matters, the not-scrambled check (0.6 to 1.6 s) is the part to look at; the real Unscramble (3 to 4 s) only runs on sets that would otherwise not play at all.
  • What broke on AltMount's addon. Whiplash and Band of Brothers S01E02 top picks (HTTP 503 after AltMount's own article check), and second or third picks on Severance and The Bear; the same two top picks failed again in the warm run. Blade Runner 2049 showed only 2 Sources because AltMount's relevance filter dropped 191 results for that title (it tripped over the "2049" in the name).
  • What broke on the other two (corrected). Most of these were not bad Sources. From 16:18 UTC DrunkenSlug refused NZB downloads with "Request limit reached" (its daily grab limit), so every DrunkenSlug pick after that showed the failure video: one The Wire, one Whiplash, two Severance and two Better Call Saul picks. The Sources stay alive (ADR 0004). Only two failures came from AltMount itself: The Wire S01 season pack ("invalid NZB: total size is zero") and a Martian release ("all files failed validation"). The Band of Brothers S01 4K pack showed the failure video on NzbDAV and played on AltMount.
